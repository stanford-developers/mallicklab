Funding

We are fortunate to participate in a number of great research initiatives.  Check them out below!

 

Agency  Program Name  Objective  
NIH / NCI Innovative Molecular Analysis Technologies Development of novel devices for studying single cell biophysical and molecular characteristics.   
NIH / NCI NCI Alliance for Nanotechnology in Cancer Development of novel markers and diagnostic devices for predicting and monitoring therapeutic response.    
NIH / NCI Clinical Proteomics Tumor Analysis Consortia Development of tools for multi-data fusion.  
NIH / NCI Physical Sciences in Oncology   Multi-scale, integrative modeling and experimentation to develop a comprehensive molecular to host model of cancer.  
NIH / NCI Consortium for Molecular and Cellular Characterization of Screen-Detected Lesions Development of approaches to predict aggressivity of prostate cancer lesions via multi-data fusion.  
NIH / NCI Alliance of Glycobiologists for Cancer Research Glycosylation and Immune Evasion in Urologic Tumors  
NIH / NLM  Big Data to Knowledge  Development of a Semantically Aware Discovery Engine for Accelerating Scientific Multi-data analysis.  
DARPA Physics of AI Integration of physics-based priors into learning architectures and to improve accuracy and decrease overfitting.   
DARPA Big Mechanism Machine reading and network modeling to predict therapeutic response.  
DARPA Simplifying Complexity in Scientific Discovery (SIMPLEX)  Automating Scientific Discovery  
DARPA Deep Purposeful Learning (Deep Purple)   Advancing AI methods for multi-modal data, particularly drawing on prior knowledge.
